# AI Fundamentals

An introductory Artificial Intelligence course designed for teenagers, combining fundamental concepts with hands-on experimentation and project-based learning.

The course begins with an overview of Artificial Intelligence and gradually introduces students to Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Large Language Models, and Prompt Engineering. Alongside the conceptual sessions, selected topics are supported by practical Jupyter notebooks.

The course concludes with two project-based sessions. Students develop their own ideas for specialized AI applications and build domain-specific chatbots using AI APIs. The final projects provide an opportunity to apply concepts from the course, experiment with AI models, and turn an idea into a working application.

## Course Topics

The course covers the following topics:

* Artificial Intelligence fundamentals
* Machine Learning
* Computer Vision
* Large Language Models (LLMs)
* Prompt Engineering
* AI APIs
* Building AI-powered applications

## Final Projects

The final two sessions are dedicated to project-based learning.

Students develop an idea for an AI-powered application and implement a specialized chatbot using an AI API. The projects are intended to provide practical experience with the process of moving from an initial idea to a functional AI application.

The projects cover concepts such as:

* Working with AI APIs
* Designing specialized chatbots
* Prompt Engineering
* Connecting an application to an AI model
* Defining a specific domain or use case
* Experimenting with and refining AI-based applications

## Learning Goals

By the end of the course, students should have a basic understanding of:

* The fundamental concepts of Artificial Intelligence
* The role of Machine Learning within AI
* The difference between supervised and unsupervised learning
* Basic concepts in Computer Vision
* The fundamental ideas behind Large Language Models
* The role of prompts in interacting with AI models
* How AI APIs can be integrated into applications
* How to design and build a simple AI-powered application
